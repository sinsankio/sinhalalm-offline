# Offline සිංහල

Offline Q&A in Sinhala Language. Ask a question in Sinhala, and [SinhalaLLM-1.7B](https://huggingface.co/namindu94/SinhalaLLM-1.7B) answers it inside your browser, with no server and no connection once the model is downloaded.

- The 8-bit model (2.48 GB) is used on computers whose browser offers WebGPU, and the 4-bit model (1.19 GB) otherwise. The model is downloaded once and kept in the browser until you delete it.
- Answers are generated by a small language model and are not verified. They may be fluent but factually wrong, and they are not suitable for medical, legal or financial decisions.
- Your questions never leave your device.

For the best speed, open the app directly at its own address rather than inside this page, in a recent Chrome or Edge with GPU acceleration. Chrome on Linux needs WebGPU to be enabled; the app explains how.
